# 15-minute user test

A short session to watch one real person use the academy and learn where they get stuck. Run it with three people from different roles before using the site with customers. Author: **Daniel Yuanpeng Xu**.

[中文版](#中文15-分钟用户测试)

## Who to invite

Three people, one from each group:

| Role | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Someone new to BPMS (a new PM, analyst or intern) | Tests the beginner path |
| Someone who builds or runs processes (developer, admin, support) | Tests the how-to guides and troubleshooting |
| Someone who talks to customers (customer success, sales engineer, PM) | Tests whether the material answers customer questions |

## Ground rules

- Use only the public site. Do not show or discuss customer names, data or internal plans.
- Ask before recording anything. Notes are enough.
- Say: "We are testing the site, not you. Please think aloud."
- Do not help unless they are stuck for more than two minutes. Note where it happened.

## Script (15 minutes)

1. **Set up (1 min).** Open <https://xudaniel.github.io/webmethods-bpms-academy/> on their phone or laptop, in the language they prefer.
2. **Task 1, beginner (4 min).** "You have never used a BPMS. Find out what it does." Success: they finish the 10-minute course and pass its three checks.
3. **Task 2, practitioner (4 min).** "You uploaded a process and it does not start. Find the fix." Success: they reach the answer about enabling the process for execution, by search, troubleshooting or the guides.
4. **Task 3, customer question (3 min).** "A customer asks: where does AI fit without losing control? Find a page you would show them." Success: they reach the agent pattern, the controls for regulated industries or the demo script.
5. **Five questions (3 min).**
   1. Where were you confused or slowed down?
   2. What would you use in your own work?
   3. Did anything look wrong or out of date?
   4. What is missing?
   5. From 0 to 10, how likely are you to share it with a colleague? Who?

## What to record

For each task: time taken, whether they succeeded, where they got stuck and one short quote. Then file the session with the [feedback form](https://github.com/Xudaniel/webmethods-bpms-academy/issues/new?template=feedback.yml), without names.

## After three sessions

Fix anything that stopped two or more people. Fix wrong facts at once and cite the source. Note the rest for the roadmap.

## 中文：15 分钟用户测试

观察一个真实的人使用学院网站，找出他们卡在哪里。拿去给客户用之前，先找三个不同角色的人各测一次。

**请谁来测：** 一位刚接触 BPMS 的人（新 PM、分析师或实习生），一位做流程开发或运维的人，一位面对客户的人（客户成功、售前或 PM）。

**规则：** 只用公开网站，不展示、不讨论客户名称、数据和内部计划；录音录屏要先征得同意，记笔记就够；开场说"我们测的是网站，不是你，请边做边说"；卡住超过两分钟再帮忙，并记下卡在哪里。

**流程（15 分钟）：**

1. **准备（1 分钟）：** 用对方的手机或电脑打开网站，选对方习惯的语言。
2. **任务 1，零基础（4 分钟）：** "你从没用过 BPMS，弄清楚它是做什么的。" 完成标准：做完 10 分钟速成并答对三道自测题。
3. **任务 2，实操（4 分钟）：** "你上传了一个流程，但它启动不了，找到解决办法。" 完成标准：通过搜索、故障排查或操作指南找到"启用执行"的答案。
4. **任务 3，客户问题（3 分钟）：** "客户问：AI 放在哪里才不会失控？找一页你会拿给客户看的内容。" 完成标准：找到 agent 模式、受监管行业的合规控制或客户演示脚本。
5. **五个问题（3 分钟）：** 哪里让你困惑或变慢？哪些内容你工作中会用？有没有看起来错误或过时的地方？缺什么？0 到 10 分，你多愿意推荐给同事？推荐给谁？

**记录：** 每个任务的用时、是否完成、卡在哪里、一句原话。之后用[反馈表](https://github.com/Xudaniel/webmethods-bpms-academy/issues/new?template=feedback.yml)提交，不写姓名。

**三次之后：** 两个人以上都卡住的地方先改；错误事实立即更正并标出处；其余记入路线图。
