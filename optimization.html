Optimization primer: how MIP and CP solvers think优化入门：MIP 和 CP 求解器是怎么想的

Rules decide what is allowed. Optimization decides what is best. A process (BPMS) carries the result out. This page explains the two algorithm families behind solvers such as IBM CPLEX Optimizer, CP Optimizer and OR-Tools, with small examples you can check by hand and three demos that run in your browser.规则决定什么可以做，优化决定什么最好，流程（BPMS）负责执行。本页讲 IBM CPLEX Optimizer、CP Optimizer、OR-Tools 这类求解器背后的两类算法，每个都有能手算核对的小例子，另有三个在浏览器里运行的演示。

Teaching material with self-written examples and public concepts only. Not an official IBM resource. Runnable C and Python code is in examples/optimization.教学材料，例子为自编，只用公开概念，不是 IBM 官方资源。可运行的 C 和 Python 代码在 examples/optimization。

Demo 1: branch and bound on a snack budget演示 1：用分支定界选零食

Cola costs 4 and gives 5 happiness; chocolate 5 and 6; chips 6 and 7. Each at most once. Move the budget and watch the solver compute a bound, try a branch, and prune.可乐 4 元 5 分，巧克力 5 元 6 分，薯片 6 元 7 分，每样最多一份。拖动预算，看求解器怎么算上限、试分支、剪枝。






Demo 2: constraint propagation on a duty roster演示 2：用约束传播排值班




Three people, A, B and C, take the duty on Mon, Tue and Wed, one each. B is the day after A. C is not on Wed. Tap Propagate first, then guess if it is not enough.甲、乙、丙三人周一到周三各值一天。乙在甲的第二天，丙不能周三。先点「传播」，不够再猜。











Demo 3: rules first, then optimization演示 3：先规则，后优化




Three electricians: Zhang San 50 an hour, Li Si 40, Wang Wu 45; at most 6 hours each. Orders: A 3 h, B 2 h, C 4 h. C is high-voltage work, and only Zhang San and Wang Wu hold the certificate. A rules engine decides who is allowed; the optimizer picks the cheapest plan among the allowed ones. Switch the rule off and on.三个电工：张三时薪 50，李四 40，王五 45，每人最多 6 小时。工单：A 3 小时，B 2 小时，C 4 小时。C 是高压活，只有张三和王五持证。规则引擎决定谁可以做，优化器在可以的人里选最省钱的方案。把规则关掉、打开对比一下。
